Solar service lines / 01

SMA & Fimer inverter repair and service.

We support SMA and Fimer (ABB) string and central solar inverters two ways — component-level repair of boards and sub-assemblies sent to our bench, and full inverter service on site, including fault-finding, replacement, commissioning and refurbished exchange units.

Send a unit or board for repair
01

Receive

A board, sub-assembly or a complete inverter — with the fault description and any error codes logged by the unit or the monitoring portal.

02

Diagnose

Bench testing to isolate the failure to a component or a stage, or on-site fault-finding where the inverter stays in place.

03

Repair

Component-level repair with matched parts; board or module exchange where a repair is not economic.

04

Test

Function and load testing against the unit's acceptance criteria, with results recorded.

05

Return / commission

The repaired item goes back with its test record, or is refitted and commissioned on site. [PLACEHOLDER — typical turnaround time.]
