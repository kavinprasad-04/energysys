Wind / service

Wind services — manufacture, refurbishment, testing & supply

EnergySYS keeps wind turbines turning — parts made to drawing, worn units brought back to spec, everything tested and certified before it ships.

Home/Service/Wind

Service lines / 01

Six things we do.

From a single machined part to a full refurbishment programme with held stock — scoped to the drawing and the turbine platform.

Talk to the workshop
S-01

Manufacturing

Machined and fabricated components to the OEM drawing or a reverse-engineered sample. Every part ships with a material certificate and a dimensional report against the controlling drawing.

S-02

Refurbishment & repair

Strip, clean, inspect, replace bearings, seals and wear parts, reassemble and test. Each unit returns with an inspection record — what was measured, what was replaced and the tests it passed.

S-03

Testing & inspection

Pressure and flow rigs for hydraulic units, electrical bench testing for slip rings and controls, dimensional and NDT checks. Results recorded and issued with the part.

S-04

Supply & held stock

Call-off stock of common spares — seal kits, fastener sets, filter elements, isolators — held against a framework agreement and shipped as matched sets.

S-05

Reverse engineering

Obsolete or unsupported part? We measure or 3D-scan a sample, produce a controlled drawing and re-manufacture to the original fit, form and function.

S-06

Field & site support

Structured failure investigation (8D), root-cause analysis and fitment guidance for site teams. Open a service / failure report →

How it runs / 03

Enquiry to shipped part.

Send a code, a drawing or a nameplate photo. We confirm scope and lead time before any work starts.

01

Enquiry

Part code, OEM number, drawing or nameplate photo — plus the turbine platform and quantity.

02

Assessment & quote

We match the part or assess the unit, confirm what's needed and quote price and lead time.

03

Manufacture / refurbish

Work proceeds against the controlling drawing with in-process inspection and hold points.

04

Test, certify & ship

Final test and dimensional check, certificates and records issued, part packed and dispatched.
