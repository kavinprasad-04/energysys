Capabilities / 01

Seven service lines, one drivetrain.

From a single seal to a full annual parts contract — configured around your turbine models and your maintenance calendar.

Full service detail
  1. 01

    Refurbishing services

    Servicing and refurbishing critical wind turbine components to extend their service life and reduce replacement costs.

  2. 02

    Rotating joints services

    Manufacturing, servicing, testing and repairing rotating joints used in wind turbines.

  3. 03

    Main shaft & bearing housing services

    Manufacturing and servicing main bearing housing assemblies — replacing bearings, seals, O-rings and related components.

  4. 04

    Power pack services

    Repairing, servicing, testing and supplying hydraulic power packs and their components.

  5. 05

    Custom designed parts

    Designing, redesigning, developing, testing and supplying customised wind turbine parts to specific requirements.

  6. 06

    Spare parts & consumables

    Supplying a wide range of maintenance parts and consumables for different wind turbine models and manufacturers.

  7. 07

    Annual parts contract

    Annual spare-parts supply contracts that help you hold the right inventory and minimise turbine downtime.
